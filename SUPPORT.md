# Support

Lucent is maintained by one person in their own time. Please pick the right channel so requests do
not get lost.

## Bug report

Something is broken or behaves incorrectly.

→ Open a **Bug report** issue.

Include your Lucent version, your Windows version, which music player you were using, and the steps
that reproduce it. A screenshot helps for anything visual.

## Player compatibility

A specific music player is not detected, shows the wrong metadata, shows no artwork, or gets the
wrong lyrics.

→ Open a **Player compatibility** issue.

This template asks for the player's `SourceAppUserModelId` and the metadata it exposes. Without that
information a compatibility report usually cannot be acted on, because the behaviour depends on what
that particular app reports to Windows.

## Feature request

You want Lucent to do something it does not do.

→ Open a **Feature request** issue.

Describe the problem you are trying to solve, not only the solution you have in mind. Check
[ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) first — it may already be planned, or deliberately out of scope.

## Usage question

You are not sure how something works.

→ Open a **Discussion** if enabled, otherwise a **Feature request** issue marked as a question.

Check the [README](README.md) first, especially *Known limitations* — several common surprises
(Windows-only, NetEase precise sync needing the desktop app, subtle glass refraction) are documented
behaviour rather than bugs.

## Security vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue.**

→ Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting.

## What to expect

There is no service-level agreement. Issues with clear reproduction steps get looked at first,
because they are the ones that can actually be fixed.
